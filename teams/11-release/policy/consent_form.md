# Consent form: your name in public text

The lab may name the people who did the work in its public text. We name you only if you say yes on this form.
You can change your answer at any time.

**Do not fill in this form in the repository.** The repository is public. Copy the form, fill it in, and send
it to TSAI in the way TSAI tells you. TSAI keeps the filled forms. G11 Release keeps only a list of the names
and choices.

## 1. Where your name may appear

The lab may use your name in:

- the weekly updates
- the public dashboard text
- the launch announcement
- the model card (the document that describes the model and how it was made)

## 2. What we will never publish

- your email address, phone number or home address
- your grades, or any problem that you had in the lab
- a photo or a recording of you, unless you sign a separate form for it

## 4. Change your mind

Tell the G11 lead or TSAI. We stop using your name in new text from that day. We remove it from the dashboard
and the repository within seven days. We cannot take back text that was already sent out, such as a posted
announcement.
